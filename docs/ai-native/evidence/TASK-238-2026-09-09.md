## S2 — Proposed settlement and market-applicability decisions (not approved)

This section is a decision draft, not an accounting policy or tax-owner sign-off. It
is intentionally written before any schema, command, API or UI change so the owning
decision can be reviewed without creating a second source of truth.

### Recommended v1 customer-settlement slice

1. Add an explicit customer receipt allocation aggregate in the Finance boundary. A
   receipt carries the Company-derived customer, document date, Company currency,
   positive amount and bank reference; allocation lines carry an explicit invoice ID
   and amount. The receipt must not accept client-supplied `masterFn`, `companyFn` or
   actor identity.
2. Allow one receipt to allocate to one or more unpaid sales invoices for the same
   customer and currency. Require the caller to send the exact line allocations; do
   not silently choose FIFO/oldest-due allocation because the current invoice schema
   has no approved payment-priority contract. A line amount is positive, uses the
   Company currency's two-decimal minor-unit boundary, and cannot exceed the current
   invoice outstanding amount under a row lock.
3. Permit partial payment. An invoice remains `unpaid` while its derived outstanding
   amount is positive and becomes `paid` only when it reaches exactly zero. The
   authoritative balance is the invoice total less governed allocation lines plus
   approved credit/debit corrections; screens and AI responses must read that result,
   not maintain an independent balance.
4. Require the sum of allocation lines to equal the receipt amount exactly after
   Decimal normalization. Reject cross-currency allocation, negative/zero lines,
   allocation above outstanding, invalid invoice/customer scope, closed periods and
   stale invoice versions. Do not add FX conversion in v1.
5. Reject overpayment rather than silently creating an on-account credit. A customer
   advance/credit balance would need a separate liability/accounting contract and is
   excluded from this slice by default.
6. Make replay idempotent by Company/document key and exact reviewed payload. The same
   request returns the existing receipt and allocations without new GL rows; a changed
   payload conflicts. Use one transaction and append-only audit evidence.
7. Reverse by an explicit, linked immutable reversal document in an open period. The
   reversal swaps the cash/AR legs, releases the allocations in the authoritative
   balance projection and cannot edit/delete the original receipt. Dependent corrections
   must be checked under the same transaction before reversal.
8. Keep Project Progress Claim Bank Receipt and supplier Payment Voucher behavior
   unchanged. The new customer receipt path must not reuse either table or broaden
   Payment Voucher into a generic settlement record.

### Pre-implementation source-of-truth impact map

The source review found that partial settlement cannot be implemented safely as an
isolated receipt insert. The current consumers below use `invoice.status = 'unpaid'`
and `invoice.totalAmount` as the receivable balance:

- [arAging.ts](../../../src/modules/finance/arAging.ts) reports
  `balanceBasis: 'unpaid_invoice_total'` and groups the full invoice total.
- [creditControl.ts](../../../src/modules/sales/creditControl.ts) sums full unpaid
  invoice totals when checking customer exposure.
- [dashboard.ts](../../../src/api/dashboard.ts) sums full unpaid invoice totals for
  open receivables.
- [analytics.ts](../../../src/modules/sales/analytics.ts) reports unpaid invoice
  totals and invoice-status values, including credit/debit-note joins.
- [financeReports.integration.test.ts](../../../src/api/financeReports.integration.test.ts)
  and [arAging.test.ts](../../../src/modules/finance/arAging.test.ts) currently assert
  the full-invoice balance basis and must change with the approved contract.

The implementation checkpoint therefore needs one authoritative
`customerInvoiceOutstandingWithin` calculation (invoice total minus posted governed
corrections and active receipt allocations, with reversal treatment) and must route
all four read consumers plus the new receipt command through it. Required regression
evidence includes partial, final, overpayment rejection, cross-currency rejection,
exact replay, changed-payload conflict, reversal, customer/Company isolation, closed
period and stale-version rollback. This is an implementation dependency identified by
source inspection, not a claim that the feature exists.
